## Course Overview
This specialized career preparation course demonstrates how to leverage cutting-edge AI tools (such as Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and automated resume screeners) to accelerate and optimize every stage of the data professional job search process.

### Core Modules Covered:
1. **AI-Powered Portfolio & Resume Crafting:** Structuring high-impact project bullet points using the Google XYZ formula (`Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]`), tailoring resumes to Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), and highlighting technical competencies in SQL, R, Python, and Tableau.
2. **AI-Assisted Networking & Outreach:** Generating personalized cold outreach messages, engaging with data community leaders on LinkedIn, and positioning portfolio artifacts.
3. **Interview Preparation & Technical Simulations:** Conducting simulated technical interviews, practicing behavioral responses using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result), and solving live data challenge scenarios.
4. **Negotiation & Career Trajectory:** Evaluating compensation packages, researching market benchmarks, and drafting professional communication.
