## Course Overview
In this course, learners discover how data analysts tell compelling stories through data visualizations using Tableau, presentation strategies, and visual design principles.

### Key Modules Covered:
1. **Visualizing Data**: Choosing the right visual format (bar charts, line charts, scatterplots, treemaps, choropleth maps) for the audience and data type.
2. **Creating Data Visualizations with Tableau**: Connecting to data sources, building interactive dashboards, managing discrete vs. continuous variables, and utilizing calculated fields.
3. **Crafting Data Stories**: Structuring narratives (Context, Conflict, Resolution), adhering to the McCandless Data Visualization principles (Information, Story, Goal, Visual Form), and avoiding cognitive overload.
4. **Delivering Effective Presentations**: Presenting to stakeholders, handling challenging Q&A sessions, ensuring accessibility (color-contrast, colorblind-safe palettes), and eliminating chart junk.
