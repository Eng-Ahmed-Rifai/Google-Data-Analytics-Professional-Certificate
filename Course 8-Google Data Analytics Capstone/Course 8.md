### Case Study: Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis
- **Business Task:** Analyze historical bike-share data to identify behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members to help design a targeted marketing strategy to convert casual riders into annual members.
- **Tools Used:** Spreadsheets, SQL (BigQuery), R (`tidyverse`, `ggplot2`, `lubridate`), and Tableau.
- **Key Findings:**
  1. Annual members primarily use bikes for weekday commutes with peak hours at 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
  2. Casual riders use bikes significantly more on weekends and during summer months for recreation, with longer trip durations.
  3. Electric bikes are increasingly popular among casual riders.
- **Strategic Recommendations:**
  1. Introduce seasonal weekend-to-annual membership conversion discounts.
  2. Launch summer digital campaigns highlighting cost savings for heavy weekend commuters.
  3. Offer perks for electric bike riders upgrading to annual memberships.
